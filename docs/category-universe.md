# CEGO Price Radar Category Universe

Version: 0.9.0

## Product boundary
CEGO Price Radar focuses on daily-life consumer products. The practical anchor is the product universe reasonably carried by Wellcome, PARKnSHOP, Mannings and Watsons.

HKTVmall may participate in comparison and New Arrival Radar only inside this universe. CEGO does not become a general HKTVmall catalogue.

## Primary groups
1. Food & drinks — fresh food, dairy, breakfast, rice/noodles/oil, sauces, canned/frozen food, water, soft drinks, juice, tea, coffee, snacks.
2. Household & home consumables — toilet paper, tissues, kitchen paper, laundry, dishwashing, cleaning, rubbish bags, food-storage consumables.
3. Personal care — oral care, hair care, body care, hand care, deodorant, feminine and adult-care consumables.
4. Beauty & skincare — masks, cleanser, skincare, moisturiser, sunscreen, makeup-removal and cotton products.
5. Pet daily supplies — dog/cat food, treats, cat litter and routine pet-care consumables.
6. Baby & family — nappies, wipes, baby wash/care and baby food.
7. Travel-life essentials — a limited extension for practical daily-use travel items such as travel-size toiletries, disposable underwear, travel socks, travel bottles, insect repellent, wipes and sanitising supplies.
8. Family/bulk packs — bulk water/drinks, tissue, laundry and pet-food packs where unit-price comparison is meaningful.

## Excluded by default
General fashion, televisions, computers, unrelated electronics, furniture, collectibles and other broad marketplace categories are outside Price Radar even when HKTVmall carries them.

## UX rule
Homepage shows a small frequent-use quick row plus an expandable category browser. Users can browse without having a specific product in mind, then search/compare, save, add to CEGO cart and hand off to the retailer.

## New Arrival rule
New Arrival Radar follows the same category universe. Records should distinguish official new-product evidence from CEGO first-seen detection. HKTVmall first-seen products outside this universe are ignored.
